# Thoth v0.2.0

## Highlights

- **Universal app.** One download runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Official prices, Claude 5 included.** The built-in rate card now follows
  Anthropic's API list prices (checked 2026-10-23) for Fable/Mythos 5 and 5.1,
  Opus 4 to 5.5, Sonnet 4 to 5.5 and Haiku 3.5, 4.5 and 5.5, with separate
  5-minute and 1-hour cache-write rates and the Haiku 5.5 long-prompt tier.
- **Each response counted once.** Cost, tokens and calls are no longer
  inflated by Claude Code's one-line-per-content-block logs.
- **Much faster scans.** A cold scan of a large history drops from about 27 s
  to under 2 s.

## Fixes

- Claude Code repeats the same `message.id` and usage on every content block
  (thinking, text, tool use). Thoth summed all of them, inflating cost, tokens
  and calls by about 1.9x on a real history. Each response is now counted once,
  and the activity breakdown classifies a response by the tools on all of its
  lines.
- Every `claude-opus-4*` model (4.5 to 4.8) was charged at Opus 4 rates
  ($15/$75 per MTok), about 3x the official $5/$25. `claude-haiku-4*` used
  Haiku 3.5 rates instead of $1/$5.
- Claude 5 model ids matched no price and showed as $0. Model ids are now
  parsed into family and version; a version newer than the table uses the
  closest known version of its family.
- Incremental reads (today summary, active-session poll) skipped a line that
  was still being written, losing that response for the day.
- Sessions in folders with spaces, dots or underscores never updated live or
  fed the burn rate.
- "7 Days" and "15 Days" covered 8 and 16 calendar days; they now count today
  as day one, like the 30-day window. The month-end projection no longer drops
  day 1 on the 31st.
- Thread-safety fixes in the background scan state.

## Performance

Synthetic benchmark (600 files, 946 MB, 240k responses; Apple M4, release build):

| Scan                          | v0.1.2  | v0.2.0  |
|-------------------------------|---------|---------|
| Full scan, cold               | 26.9 s  | 1.83 s  |
| Full scan, 10 files grew      | 530 ms  | 64 ms   |
| Today summary, cold           | 2.59 s  | 0.51 s  |
| Today summary, append         | 415 ms  | 101 ms  |
| Full scan, warm               | 34 ms   | 34 ms   |

With four 118 MB files, the cold scan goes from 13.5 s to 0.93 s and the scan's
extra memory from +190 MB to +32 MB.

How: only lines that carry `usage` are JSON-decoded, files are read in 4 MB
chunks, timestamps are parsed directly, and changed files are parsed in
parallel on a cold start. Reproduce with
`THOTH_BENCH=1 swift test -c release -Xswiftc -enable-testing --filter ScanBenchmark`.

## Compatibility

- Universal binary (`arm64` + `x86_64`): runs natively on Macs with Apple
  silicon and on Intel-based Macs, with no need for Rosetta. In Finder's Get
  Info it shows as made for both a Mac with Apple silicon and a Mac with an
  Intel processor (see [Apple: If you need to install Rosetta on your
  Mac](https://support.apple.com/102527)). v0.1.x was Apple silicon only.
- Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
- Still not notarised: on first launch, allow it in **System Settings >
  Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** (macOS 15+) or right-click > **Open**
  (macOS 14). See the README.

## Upgrade notes

- **Costs shown will be lower than in v0.1.x.** Responses are no longer
  counted about twice, and Opus 4.5 to 4.8 usage is priced at the official
  rates (about a third of what v0.1.x used). Your history did not change; the old
  numbers were overstated. Usage of models v0.1.x didn't recognise (Claude 5)
  now has a cost instead of $0, and Haiku 4.x is slightly higher.
- Costs are API-equivalent estimates, not what a Claude subscription bills you.
- "7 Days" and "15 Days" are now exactly 7 and 15 days, including today.
- Replace the old app: quit Thoth, drag the new `Thoth.app` to
  `/Applications` and confirm the replacement.
